All files under `assets/sprites/` and `assets/audio/` that ship with this
repository are **original work created for this project** (pixel sheets and
simple synthesized SFX/BGM). They are dedicated to the public domain under
**CC0 1.0 Universal**.

They are **not** dumps or derivatives of NES *Ninja Gaiden* / 忍者龙剑传 /
Tecmo / Konami / Koei Tecmo copyrighted graphics or music.

## Regenerating

From the repo root:

```
python3 tools/generate_assets.py
```

Overwrite any generated PNG/WAV with your own drop-in replacements of the
same filename. Third-party packs you add yourself should be listed here with
their license (CC0 / CC-BY / etc.).
